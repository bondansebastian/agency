# Indonesian law: where to start

A map of the statutes that usually apply to a drafted document. It is not a source. Resolve and read every article in Pasal.id (`resolve_law`, then `read_law`) before relying on it or citing it, and check the status first: the right-hand column only says what was read when this file was written.

| Topic | Statute (use as the `resolve_law` reference) | Articles read in Pasal.id | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valid agreement, freedom of contract, good faith | KUHPerdata (Burgerlijk Wetboek) | Pasal 1320, 1338 | Pasal.id holds an unofficial translation; say so |
| Language of agreements | UU 24 tahun 2009; Perpres 63 tahun 2019 | Pasal 31; Pasal 26 | Indonesian is mandatory with an Indonesian party; a foreign party adds its language and/or English |
| Stamp duty | UU 10 tahun 2020 (Bea Meterai) | Pasal 3, 5 | Fixed rate on a private agreement |
| Electronic contracts and signatures | UU 11 tahun 2008 (ITE), amended by UU 1 tahun 2024 | not read | Status is not certain in Pasal.id; read the amendments |
| Personal data | UU 27 tahun 2022 (PDP) | not read | Verified source |
| Company founding, directors' authority | UU 40 tahun 2007 (PT) | Pasal 7 | A PT is founded by notarial deed; status is `diubah`, read the amendments |
| Fiducia security | UU 42 tahun 1999 | Pasal 5 | Notarial deed in Indonesian |
| Arbitration and ADR | UU 30 tahun 1999 | Pasal 2, 3, 9 | The arbitration clause must be explicit |
| Standard-form clauses with consumers | UU 8 tahun 1999 (Perlindungan Konsumen) | Pasal 18 | Listed clauses are void by law; verified source |
| Employment agreements | UU 13 tahun 2003 and UU 6 tahun 2023 (Cipta Kerja), plus their implementing regulations | not read | Both are `diubah`; find the implementing regulation with `search_legal` |

For a topic not in the table (land, intellectual property, financial services, a sector licence, a regional rule), use `search_legal` with plain Indonesian legal terms, no AND/OR, and a `regulation_types` or `region` filter when it helps.
